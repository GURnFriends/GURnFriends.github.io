Workflow

GUR Process

1. Stakeholder Meeting

We meet with you to discuss your game’s design intent and research objectives based on its current state.

We establish playtest goals, design intent, and study structure.

2. Expert Review & Playthrough

We play your game and provide feedback on early UX issues and bugs prior to external testing.

This includes a UX and usability evaluation focused on identifying friction points.

This step ensures participants focus on research objectives rather than issues already known during development.

3. Playtest Execution

We recruit participants from your target audience and run structured playtests aligned with your objectives.

Data is gathered through gameplay observation, surveys, interviews, and other research methods.

4. Reporting & Delivery

We deliver a recorded slide deck containing actionable insights tailored to your playtest objectives.

You can revisit the report at any time to guide design improvements.

What You Get

  • Playtest report with actionable insights to improve your game
  • Participant recordings and gameplay clips
  • UX evaluation highlighting usability issues before external testing
  • Cleaned and organized research data
